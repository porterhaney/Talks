## Research behind his teaching

- Sivanathan & Galinsky: *Power and Overconfidence*. Feeling powerful makes negotiators overconfident, and that costs them in the deal.
- Fast, Sivanathan, Mayer & Galinsky (2012): *Power and overconfident decision-making*
- Sivanathan et al. (2008): self-affirmation as a way to reverse escalation of commitment
- Gunia, Sivanathan & Galinsky (2009): *Vicarious entrapment* (your sunk costs lead me to escalate)
- Dilution-effect paper in *Nature Human Behaviour* (drug side-effect ads)
