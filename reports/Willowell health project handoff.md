## Key findings

**The cliff**
- For a single adult, "free healthcare" almost certainly means Medicaid, which ends at **$1,835.50 a month gross** (about $22,026 a year). That's roughly 30 hours a week at about $14/hr, so the likely affected staff are the 75% roles.
- The cliff is shallow for singles. Just above it, a silver plan costs about $50–75 a month after federal and Vermont subsidies, with a $2,700 out-of-pocket maximum (Silver 87).
- **Rule:** never move someone off Medicaid with a small raise. Raises of $1–2K can leave them worse off; raises of $4K+ leave them clearly ahead.
- **Other lines for singles (2026):**
  - 200% FPL, $31,300: out-of-pocket maximum jumps to about $10,150 on standard silver.
  - 300% FPL, $46,950: Vermont Premium Assistance ends.
  - 400% FPL, $62,600: all federal premium help ends.
- **Federal subsidies:** the enhanced federal subsidies expired 12/31/2025. A House extension passed in January 2026 but the Senate hadn't acted as of September. Verify this before citing.
- **H.R.1:** Medicaid renewals move to every six months, and work requirements start in January/March 2027.

**What doesn't exist or doesn't work**
- No Vermont state health option for early educators outside public schools.
- No chamber medical plan; those closed in 2019. The chamber offers dental and vision only, through VACEplus.
- No funder pays health premiums for a single program.
- No public evidence that any Burlington peer actually pays 90% of premiums.
- A QSEHRA mostly replaces subsidy money staff already receive, so it adds little.
- **Willowell must not:**
  - pay direct primary care memberships directly
  - pay or earmark individual premiums
  - run a premium fund it controls

  All three risk counting as an employer health plan, which can block staff subsidies and trigger penalties.

**The recommended approach: two paths, each staffer chooses**
- **Stay on Medicaid**, plus benefits that don't count as income:
  - a SIMPLE IRA used as a "cliff dial" (pre-tax deferrals)
  - the federal Saver's Match, up to $1,000/yr from 2027 (verify thresholds)
  - §127 student-loan payments, up to $5,250/yr tax-free
  - dental and vision through VACEplus
  - tax-free job-required gear
  - free Medicaid paperwork help from the Office of the Health Care Advocate (1-800-917-7787)
- **Leap off Medicaid** with a raise of $4K or more, ideally by converting a 75% role to full time. Above $31,300, staff buy a ~$0 bronze plan and Willowell funds an HSA with $2–3K/yr. Staff can use it for Fiddlehead Family Health Care in Bristol (direct primary care, about $75–115/mo).
- **Cost:** about $45K/yr for 10 staff before raises.
- **Funding:**
  - Retiring the building loan frees $29,685/yr.
  - CCFAP (the state child care subsidy) could add an estimated $20–50K/yr. It pays $439/wk full-time and $240/wk part-time, versus Addison County median tuition of $300/wk. Families up to 575% FPL qualify.
  - Watch the UPK hours deduction: a 2:30 end time drops a child to the part-time rate, while extended care to 4:30 restores full-time.
- **Compliance due now:** Vermont Saves. Employers with 2+ staff and no retirement plan have owed a $75/employee/yr penalty since Oct 1, 2026. A SIMPLE IRA satisfies it. For a Jan 1 start, the election notice goes out in early November.
- **Long-term campaign goal:** a fully employer-paid, employee-only plan at about $10–14K per person per year, with the §45R tax credit for two years. This is the "every teacher can stay" goal.
